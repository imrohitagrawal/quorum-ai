## What the owner has decided

- **The shape** (approved plan of 2026-08-25, "Settled decisions" §5, quoted
  in the register): N=1 ships as a distinct "Quick answer" mode that keeps
  the answer, source support and citation coverage, the safety notices and
  the cost receipt; and drops the agreement ring, the verdict band, the
  debate transcript and convergence-based trust.
- **Its place in the order:** after W4 (the 2026-08-31 prompt lists W5 as
  "blocked on W4"; W4 is now shipped in three pull requests).
- **What it must not do** (CHG-011 D9, 2026-09-23): add a per-run choice
  before the confirmation token binds the slot list and the shape. **Met on
  2026-09-24 (ADR-0123):** the token binds the ordered panel and the priced
  shape as a string, so W5's `mode: "quick"` fits the same field.

## What is still owed by the owner — three decisions

1. **The guard.** Today `_validate_model_id_list` accepts 2 to 4
   ("Between 2 and 4 model slots are required."); `MIN_SLOT_COUNT = 2`
   (ADR-0120). Is N=1 reached by lowering the floor to 1 under a mode flag,
   or by a separate request shape (`mode: "quick"`) that never touches the
   panel validator? Both are buildable; they differ in what the API promises
   and in what W6/W20 (a panel of one reporting consensus, both fixed by
   refusing N=1) have to re-guard.
2. **The copy.** The product describes four (D3). What does a quick answer
   call itself on the landing, in the composer and on its result page, and
   does it carry the panel subline ("four models by default, three or two")
   at all? No sentence for N=1 has been written by the owner.
3. **The price posture.** A quick answer has no debate and no synthesis
   stage; does it run the judge (a priced `by_stage` row today), and does it
   keep the per-run cost gate? The estimator prices the debate and synthesis
   from `len(model_slots)` since #493; at N=1 those stages are absent, not
   cheaper, and no owner statement covers whether the mode is sold on price.
   (The session's standing recommendation — never "half", never a range in
   the headline — carries over.)
